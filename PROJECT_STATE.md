**Post-Phase-1 fix (2026-08-31, sticky-nav full-bleed bug):** user reported "the menu ... when you scroll down it looks terrible, only the small part where the buttons are is the only section going down." Root cause: `<nav className="nav shell">` combined the sticky/background/z-index container and the max-width (1240px) "shell" content wrapper on the SAME element — since `.shell` sets an explicit `width` (not `max-width`), the nav's own box (and its background/shadow) was only 1240px wide and centered, not full viewport width. On viewports wider than ~1288px, once a full-width dark section (`.proof-band`, `.ai-section`, `.contact`, `footer`) scrolled to sit behind the sticky nav, its dark background bled through in the ~150px+ margins on both sides of the nav, while only the centered 1240px strip (containing the logo/links/buttons) kept the light nav background — exactly matching the user's description. Fixed by splitting the structure in `app/site-nav.tsx` + `app/globals.css`: `<nav>` is now a plain full-width sticky/background/shadow container with no width constraint; a new inner `<div className="shell nav-inner">` holds the grid layout (brand, links, CTA, mobile toggle) and carries the 1240px max-width constraint. `.nav-panel` (mobile menu overlay) is unaffected — it was always `position:fixed; left:0; right:0`, independent of the shell. Verified by reproducing the exact bug (scrolled nav to y=0 with `.proof-band` behind it) before and after: nav `getBoundingClientRect().width` went from 1240px (bug) to 1551px, matching `window.innerWidth` (fixed) — confirmed visually via screenshot, no more dark bleed at the edges. Re-verified mobile hamburger menu still opens/closes correctly with the new markup. Lint/build clean, added `.nav-inner > .button{justify-self:end}` (was `.nav > .button`) and `.nav-inner{grid-template-columns:1fr auto auto;gap:12px}` (moved from `.nav`) at the 980px breakpoint to match the new structure.
